About me

Hi, I’m Shinuo Ma, a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. T-H. Hubert Chan.

Before joining HKU, I spent three years at East China Normal University and two years at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, earning my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in mathematics, respectively.
